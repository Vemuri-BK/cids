WHAT THE CODE MAKES THE COUNCIL LEARN

1. Four randomly initialized small U-Nets learn complete segmentation from
   separate Haar groups and the same two maps. There is no pretrained finder
   weight transfer and no S3d output skip connection.
2. The gate combines only those expert logits. Finder and S3d outputs are not
   explicit fallback members of the final mixture.
3. A 96-mm local patch does not directly contain all candidate-level/global
   evidence that S3d uses for selection.
4. Augmented map channels double as distillation targets. A dropped teacher
   means zero rather than unavailable. Both are dropped with probability 2%
   per patch under the default independent 10%/20% dropout draws.
5. SAM evidence is also zero outside processed crop coverage. This absence is
   treated as background confidence by the current teacher loss.
6. The agreement rule abs(p_f-p_c)<0.5 accepts, for example, 0.55 versus 0.10,
   producing teacher target 0.325 although binary decisions disagree.
7. Most agreement voxels can be easy background. At epoch 40 the KD term is
   only ~0.0172 after its 0.3 weight, versus main loss ~0.2826. It is worth
   ablating, but scalar magnitude does not establish its gradient importance
   or that it is the dominant problem.

DIAGNOSTIC TRAPS

- Expert Dice is raw, while council Dice is after rel20. At epoch 40 experts
  are .642/.650/.692/.660; do not interpret the entire council gap as a gate
  gain until postprocessing is matched.
- Gate-balancing loss averages over all voxels; printed gate weights use a
  predicted foreground region. Their differing behavior is not a bug proof.
- S6 validation overlap defaults to 0.25, while test uses 0.5. The finder maps
  for validation use 0.5. Reevaluate shortlisted checkpoints at 0.5 first.
- The debugger warning is unrelated to segmentation quality. The scalar-loss
  warning calls for detached logging, not an architectural change.
- S4 was not used in this run; these logs cannot judge whether its adapter helps.
- The attachment cannot show whether missing tumour, false-positive volume,
  boundary erosion, or a specific collection drives the council's losses.
  Per-case predictions and matched metrics are the next necessary diagnostics.

PRIORITY DIAGNOSIS (HYPOTHESES, NOT PROVEN CAUSES)

Most promising: preserve S3d's global candidate decision and learn corrections.
Next: separate teacher evidence from map corruption and missing coverage.
Next: address optimistic in-sample upstream maps and difficult-error sampling.
Then: add image context to frequency specialists and evaluate gate usefulness.
Lower priority: larger models, stronger balancing, metadata, or longer training
before the above comparisons establish what is limiting performance.
