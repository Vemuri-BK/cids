3. EXACT LIST OF 25 FEATURES WE EXTRACT IN `cids/radiomics.py`
================================================================================
To prevent scanner domain shift across DUKE, ISPY1, ISPY2, and NACT, all intensity
features are self-normalized against the patient's own local peritumoral ring or
whole-breast foreground (`fg = post1 > 0`).

A. 3D MORPHOLOGICAL & VASCULARITY RADIOMICS (5 features — Rejects Blood Vessels)
   Computed from 3D voxel coordinates and binary surface of candidate `comp`:
   1. `rad_elongation`     : sqrt(lambda_2 / lambda_1) from 3D inertia tensor eigenvalues
                             (lambda_1 >= lambda_2 >= lambda_3). Tubular vessels -> ~0.1-0.3;
                             focal tumours -> ~0.6-1.0.
   2. `rad_flatness`       : sqrt(lambda_3 / lambda_1). Thin sheets/vessels -> low; 3D masses -> high.
   3. `rad_sphericity`     : (pi^(1/3) * (6 * V)^(2/3)) / A, where V = volume, A = 3D surface area.
   4. `rad_solidity`       : V / V_bbox (fraction of 3D bounding box filled; tortuous diagonal
                             vessels have solidity < 0.15; solid tumours have 0.35 - 0.70).
   5. `rad_surf_to_vol`    : A / V (3D surface-to-volume ratio in mm^-1).

B. 3D ANATOMICAL LOCATION & MULTIFOCAL PRIORS (4 features — Rejects Lymph Nodes & Heart)
   Computed relative to the patient's breast foreground bounding box (`fg = post1 > 0`):
   6. `rad_ap_depth`       : Normalized anterior-to-posterior depth inside the body.
                             (Heart, internal thoracic vessels, and axillary lymph nodes sit
                             much deeper posteriorly than breast parenchyma!)
   7. `rad_lr_midline`     : Normalized distance from the medial chest midline (|x - 0.5| * 2).
   8. `rad_si_pos`         : Normalized superior-to-inferior coordinate (axillary tail is superior).
   9. `rad_dist_to_dom_mm` : 3D Euclidean distance (mm) from candidate k to the patient's
                             dominant (largest / highest-prob) candidate.
                             (0 mm = dominant; < 20 mm = satellite lobe of multifocal tumour;
                             > 60 mm = distant/contralateral false positive).

C. CONTRAST KINETICS & PERITUMORAL RING RATIOS (6 features — Rejects BPE & Cysts)
   Uses a 4 mm 3D peritumoral parenchymal ring: `ring = dilate(comp, 4 mm) & ~comp & fg`,
   and a 2 mm internal boundary rim: `rim = comp & ~erode(comp, 2 mm)`, `core = erode(comp, 2 mm)`:
   10. `rad_lesion_ring_cnr`   : (mean_in(sub) - mean_ring(sub)) / (std_ring(sub) + 0.01).
                                 Local Contrast-to-Noise Ratio! True tumours stand out sharply
                                 above their 4 mm ring; diffuse BPE blends into its ring (CNR ~ 0).
   11. `rad_lesion_ring_ratio` : (mean_in(post1) + 0.01) / (mean_ring(post1) + 0.01).
   12. `rad_washin_mean`       : Mean relative enhancement `(post1 - pre) / (pre + 0.05)` inside `comp`.
   13. `rad_washin_p90`        : 90th-percentile peak angiogenic wash-in inside `comp`.
   14. `rad_rim_core_ratio`    : (mean_rim(sub) + 0.01) / (mean_core(sub) + 0.01).
                                 Captures malignant rim enhancement (e.g., triple-negative breast
                                 cancer with central necrosis) vs. uniform vessel lumen.
   15. `rad_breast_z_sub`      : Candidate mean `sub` z-scored against the whole breast `fg`.

D. 3D TEXTURE, HETEROGENEITY & MARGIN SHARPNESS (5 features)
   16. `rad_sub_entropy`       : Shannon histogram entropy (16 bins) of `sub` inside `comp`.
   17. `rad_sub_iqr`           : Interquartile range (p75 - p25) of `sub` inside `comp`.
   18. `rad_sub_skew`          : Skewness of `sub` distribution inside `comp`.
   19. `rad_edge_sharpness`    : Mean 3D Sobel gradient magnitude `|grad(post1)|` on the candidate
                                 surface divided by the local ring gradient magnitude.
   20. `rad_seg_entropy`       : Mean binary entropy of SegResNet probability `sprob` inside `comp`.

E. WITHIN-PATIENT RELATIVE NORMALIZATION (5 features — Rescues Low-Contrast NACT/1.5T Cases)
   Normalizes candidate k against the strongest candidate in the SAME patient examination:
   21. `rel_seg_prob`          : `seg_prob_mean / max_in_case(seg_prob_mean)`
   22. `rel_sam_soft`          : `(sam_soft_ml + 0.01) / (max_in_case(sam_soft_ml) + 0.01)`
   23. `rel_agree`             : `agree - max_in_case(agree)`
   24. `rel_mem_knn`           : `mem_knn - max_in_case(mem_knn)`
   25. `rel_ring_cnr`          : `rad_lesion_ring_cnr - max_in_case(rad_lesion_ring_cnr)`

4. WHAT WE CAN BUILD & THE EXACT EXPERIMENTS FOR THE IEEE PAPER
================================================================================

We will create 3 clean, self-contained files (without breaking any existing files):

1. `cids/radiomics.py`
   - Fast pure-NumPy/SciPy 3D radiomic extractor (crops a tight bbox + 6 mm margin around
     each candidate so each candidate takes ~5 milliseconds on CPU!).
   - `CrossFitRadiomicJury`: Patient-grouped 5-fold cross-fitting (`GroupKFold`) that produces
     out-of-fold `rad_probe`, `rad_trees`, and `rad_knn` scores without data leakage.
   - `biomarker_concordance(pred_rad_df, gt_rad_df)`: Computes ICC(3,1), Lin's CCC, Spearman r,
     and relative volume error for the Clinical Utility section.

2. `scripts/s3h_radiomics_jury.py`
   - Reads your existing `s3f_jury` directory (`train/val/test_features.csv`, `*_desc.npy`,
     and `cases/<cid>.npz`) + the 2-channel MRI cache (`~/data`).
   - Extracts and caches `train_radiomics.csv`, `val_radiomics.csv`, `test_radiomics.csv`
     with SHA-256 manifest verification (takes ~2-3 minutes on CPU using multiprocessing).
   - Evaluates the complete, controlled IEEE Ablation Table (choosing thresholds strictly on
     Validation 120, evaluating once on Official Test 306):
       * Row 1: `seg_rel20` (Finder + morphological heuristic: 0.6835 Test Dice)
       * Row 2: `segjury_0.7` (Finder + Checker hand rule: 0.6992 Test Dice)
       * Row 3: `jury_rad_only` (Finder + 3D Radiomics Verifier alone — no SAM!)
       * Row 4: `jury11` (S3d: Finder + SAM Checker cues: 0.7352 Test Dice)
       * Row 5: `jury13` (S3f: Finder + SAM Checker + SAM Visual Memory: 0.7495 Test Dice)
       * Row 6: `jury_hybrid_rad_mem` (Proposed CIDS: Finder + SAM Memory + 3D Radiomics + Relative Clues)
       * Row 7: `jury_hybrid_fused` (+ Radiomics-guided Finder-Checker probability fusion on kept candidates)
   - Outputs:
       * Full 3D metrics (`Dice`, `HD95 median/mean`, `NSD@2mm`, `Missed < 0.1`, per-cohort breakdown,
         paired Wilcoxon p-values, and 95% bootstrap CIs).
       * Feature Importance / Ablation by Radiomic Family (Shape vs. Anatomy vs. Kinetics vs. Texture)
         and False-Positive Rejection Breakdown (how many elongated vessels, deep lymph nodes, and
         low-CNR BPE blobs were rejected!).
       * Downstream Clinical Radiomic Biomarker Concordance Table (ICC & CCC of Volume, Sphericity,
         Elongation, Wash-In Enhancement, Rim-to-Core Ratio, and Entropy against Expert GT).

3. `tests/test_radiomics.py`
   - Unit tests verifying:
       * Sphere vs. cylinder/tube `rad_elongation` and `rad_sphericity`.
       * Peritumoral 4 mm ring CNR and rim-to-core ratio on synthetic enhancing lesions.
       * Zero patient leakage in `GroupKFold` cross-fitted radiomic scores.
       * Exact ICC and CCC concordance math.
